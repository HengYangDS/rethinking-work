# Brief for a reader of changed passages

Give this file and the Skill's path to a reader who did not write the passages. The reader edits nothing.

Read the passages as a member of a quantitative data department reads a book written for them by their department
head, in Chinese. Read every sentence; do not summarize. Mark each place where:

- **A, translated or wrong Chinese**: a word not in its dictionary sense, a collocation a careful Chinese editor would
  not print, English word order, a participle or passive carried over, a circumlocution where `terms.md` has a name.
- **B, cold toward people**: a person treated as an item processed, purpose missing before a rule about people, a
  shortfall without the support that answers it, mechanism out of proportion (`human-voice.md`).
- **C, unlike people**: a scene, number or behaviour that colleagues would not produce, against custom or common sense.
- **D, unclear**: a sentence that must be read twice, an actor or referent left to guess.
- **E, word and composition**: a word that is not the most exact one, a near repetition, stacked modifiers, an
  unbalanced parallel, a paragraph without one point, a missing or mechanical transition, a section out of the order a
  reader needs, a passage out of proportion, an ending that trails (`composition.md`).

Do not mark a sentence only because another wording is possible. Facts, numbers, commands and quoted words are not to
be changed; if a fix would change one, say so instead of proposing it.

Report as your final message, one line per place: `file:line`, the quoted words, the letter, the proposed wording, and
one clause on why. End with the three places that most need attention.
