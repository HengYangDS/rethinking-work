## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Every place judged an error is corrected, with the least wording that fixes it.

**Non-Goals:**

- The readers' notes that were not judged errors (`notes-claude.tsv`, `notes-codex.tsv`); they are left for the
  editors' decisions with the sample's notes.

## Decisions

- Chapter 29's 「在岗的年限、经历的时间」 becomes 「资历的长短」: DDWG's "years, calendar time" names one span twice, and the
  book keeps its meaning without the overlap.
- The permission table's columns are 1.2 : 0.95 : 2.05 and two cells are shortened, so the long option sets on one line
  without a lone character; the first cell takes two lines so that the table, split at a page foot, keeps its least
  part on each page.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- The changed passages are read again before the sample.
