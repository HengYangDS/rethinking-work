# Proposal

## Why

The Chinese handbook needs a durable publication owner, not another folder of PDFs and temporary scripts. Its sources must support a general reader while retaining the exact cases, limitations and teaching fixtures behind its claims.

## What Changes

- Form a semantic source tree for the manuscript, publication design, diagram sources, exercises and public references.
- Migrate the current candidate without silently rewriting examples or discarding old editions.
- Centralize title, author Heng Yang (衡阳), date and PDF metadata.
- Expose native build, render, format and check tasks using existing installed tools.
- Keep generated artifacts, machine-specific receipts and recovery objects outside the tracked publication source.
- Continue the general-reader editorial revision and publication acceptance under this Change, with no parallel checklist.
- Publish the accepted source to the authorized private collaboration and public distribution peers, with explicit peer declarations and separate exact-commit verification.
- Add shared-source native CI for formatting, pipeline consistency and the synthetic exercise contracts; retain PDF qualification and editorial acceptance as separate obligations.
- Extend the public macOS job with the native PDF and `.tgz` build using complete declared fonts and publisher packages; qualify actual independent-machine artifacts separately from source checks.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `publication-source`: maintain an attributable, navigable publication and build it from complete declared source inputs.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This is a new publication repository.

## Impact

Only this repository, its authored publication inputs and its two authorized forge destinations are affected. Destination identity, visibility and publication rights must be resolved before creating a repository or pushing source. No model calls, new accounts, live services, host upgrades or foreign repositories are included. Existing editions and unique evidence retain their original custody until migration acceptance releases each binding.
