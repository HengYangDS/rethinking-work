# Proposal

## Why

The Chinese handbook needs a durable publication owner, not another folder of PDFs and temporary scripts. Its sources must support a general reader while retaining the exact cases, limitations and teaching fixtures behind its claims.

## What Changes

- Form a semantic source tree for the manuscript, publication design, diagram sources, exercises and public references.
- Migrate the current candidate without silently rewriting examples or discarding old editions.
- Centralize title, author Heng Yang (衡阳), date and PDF metadata.
- Expose native build, render, format and check tasks using existing installed tools.
- Keep generated artifacts, machine-specific receipts and recovery objects outside the tracked publication source.
- Continue the general-reader editorial revision and publication acceptance under this Change, with no parallel checklist.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `publication-source`: maintain an attributable, navigable publication and build it from complete declared source inputs.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This is a new publication repository.

## Impact

Only the new repository and this task's authored publication inputs are affected. No model calls, accounts, live services, host upgrades, foreign repositories or remote publication are included. Existing editions and unique evidence retain their original custody until migration acceptance releases each binding.
