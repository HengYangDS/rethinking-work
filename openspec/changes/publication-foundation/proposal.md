# Proposal

## Why

The Chinese book needs a durable publication owner, not another folder of PDFs and temporary scripts. Its sources must carry the department leader's account of work in the agentic era while retaining the worked cases, limits and teaching fixtures behind its claims.

The 37-chapter edition formed here is organized by the author's tool estate. A first rebuild turned it into a data-craft handbook. The owner rejected both on 2026-11-14: the first for shallow argument and weak grounding, the second for aiming too low. The book is about work and team collaboration in the agentic era, written from the leader's position to teach the team how to think, act and collaborate, and so to reshape how it works.

## What Changes

- Form a semantic source tree for the manuscript, publication design, diagram sources, exercises and public references.
- Migrate the current candidate without silently rewriting examples or discarding old editions.
- Centralize title, author Heng Yang (衡阳), date and PDF metadata.
- Expose native build, render, format and check tasks using existing installed tools.
- Keep generated artifacts, machine-specific receipts and recovery objects outside the tracked publication source.
- **BREAKING**: Rebuild the manuscript as the leader's teaching account, ordered along one line: the root (what work is and what holds), then how understanding is reached by one person, after handing work to an Agent (what an Agent is, layer by layer, then handing out and taking back), and by several people, with four practice modules and companion exercises, before returning to the person. Tool tours, workstation inventories and step-by-step data craft leave the book; data work remains as worked cases. Earlier editions stay in source history and in their delivered artifacts.
- Continue editorial revision and publication acceptance under this Change, with no parallel checklist.
- Publish the accepted source to the authorized private collaboration and public distribution peers, with explicit peer declarations and separate exact-commit verification.
- Add shared-source native CI for formatting, pipeline consistency and the synthetic exercise contracts; retain PDF qualification and editorial acceptance as separate obligations.
- Extend the public macOS job with the native PDF and `.tgz` build using complete declared fonts and publisher packages; qualify actual independent-machine artifacts separately from source checks.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `publication-source`: maintain an attributable, navigable publication and build it from complete declared source inputs.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This is a new publication repository.

## Impact

Only this repository, its authored publication inputs and its two authorized forge destinations are affected. The rebuild replaces the manuscript modules, the figure set and the manuscript-specific publication tests; companion exercise fixtures keep their bytes and behavior. The book discloses no internal organization detail, name, vendor, system or performance figure. A separate publication for Agent workstation operation is out of scope. Destination identity, visibility and publication rights must be resolved before creating a repository or pushing source. No model calls, new accounts, live services, host upgrades or foreign repositories are included. Existing editions and unique evidence retain their original custody until migration acceptance releases each binding.
