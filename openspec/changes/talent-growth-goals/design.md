## Context

Chapter 29 followed the department's guidelines closely enough to be used as a procedure. The owner, on 2026-11-28, decided its purpose: helping members grow, with clear goals, not
measuring people; principles and spirit in the book, procedure in the guidelines; and, when a draft left the grades and
tiers out altogether, that they stay, explained as goals. A survey of Chinese classics on guiding, knowing and judging
people (Daoist, Confucian and Legalist texts, read from Wikisource with their commentaries) informed the positioning
and the narration.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** a chapter a leader and a member can both read for where the member stands and where to go next; the grades
and tiers stated as goals; the principles of assessment kept; no person treated as an object.

**Non-Goals:** changing any rule of the guidelines; the book-wide pass for wording that treats people as objects, which
follows in its own Change; a release.

## Decisions

- **Purpose before mechanism.** The chapter opens on a period-end conversation and the four things a team does for a
  member's growth (state the goal, see what was done, recognise it, agree the next step); the grades and tiers follow as
  the goals that conversation refers to.
- **Grades in prose, by stage.** Each stage states its goal and what its two grades ask, in the guidelines' words
  shortened; a table would set the eight grades as gates to pass, while the stage's goal is what joins them.
- **Tiers read from B, each with its next step.** B is the grade's expected performance, C the pass, D below it; A and
  S presuppose B and look at the next grade. One sentence gives the next step each tier points to. The conditions in
  detail stay in the guidelines, so the book keeps one source of each.
- **成色 instead of the two sides.** The three things chapter 31 says stay with a person (弄明白、担得起、让别人也弄得明白)
  are what the chapter looks for, so the book names them once and both chapters agree.
- **Classics, three and exact.** 《传习录》 (成色, not 分两) because volume and speed are what an agent multiplies;
  《学记》 (道而弗牵) for guiding; 《道德经》 17 (王弼本) for the end. Not used: 57's 「法令滋彰」, which only the 王弼本
  reads so (the 河上公本, 郭店 and 帛书 have 法物); the 「九征」 of 《庄子·列御寇》, which tests people by traps; 「君子不器」,
  which would read as belittling the first stage.
- **Baselines as the guidelines hold them.** The four baselines of the guidelines are stated as three points (evidence,
  bounds, someone answers for the result), two of which are chapter 3's.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- A reader who needs a tier's conditions in detail must open the guidelines; the chapter says where.
- The classics are new to the book's register; each is glossed in plain Chinese and used once.
