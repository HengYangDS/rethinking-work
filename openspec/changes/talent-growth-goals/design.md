## Context

Chapter 29 followed the department's guidelines closely enough to be used as a procedure. The owner, on 2026-11-28, decided its purpose: helping members grow, with clear goals, not
measuring people; principles and spirit in the book, procedure in the guidelines; and, when a draft left the grades and
tiers out altogether, that they stay, explained as goals. A survey of Chinese classics on guiding, knowing and judging
people (Daoist, Confucian and Legalist texts, read from Wikisource with their commentaries) informed the positioning
and the narration.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** a chapter a leader and a member can both read for where the member stands and where to go next; the grades
and tiers stated as goals; the principles of assessment kept; no person treated as an object.

**Non-Goals:** changing any rule of the guidelines; 带宽 as a term, which names how much one person can understand in
thirty-one places and reads correctly there (a term changes everywhere or nowhere), so only the sentences that
allocated other people's understanding as a capacity are recast; the department's case facts in the leading chapter; a
release.

## Decisions

- **Purpose before mechanism.** The chapter opens on a period-end conversation and the four things a team does for a
  member's growth (state the goal, see what was done, recognise it, agree the next step); the grades and tiers follow as
  the goals that conversation refers to.
- **Grades in prose, by stage.** Each stage states its goal and what its two grades ask, in the guidelines' words
  shortened; a table would set the eight grades as gates to pass, while the stage's goal is what joins them.
- **Tiers read from B, each with its next step.** B is the grade's expected performance, C the pass, D below it; A and
  S presuppose B and look at the next grade. One sentence gives the next step each tier points to. The conditions in
  detail stay in the guidelines, so the book keeps one source of each.
- **成色 instead of the two sides.** The three things chapter 31 says stay with a person (弄明白、担得起、让别人也弄得明白)
  are what the chapter looks for, so the book names them once and both chapters agree.
- **One classical line, Daoist.** A survey of Daoist, Confucian and Legalist texts informed the chapter; the owner
  then asked that quotations not be overused and said he prefers Daoist ones. The chapter keeps one, at its close: 《道德经》 33,
  「知人者智，自知者明。胜人者有力，自胜者强。」 (王弼本; the gloss's 才是 follows 王弼's note, 「未若自知者超智之上也」). Knowing
  oneself answers the chapter's question, and overcoming oneself rather than others is its principle of not comparing
  members with one another; the member's period shows both. It replaced 17's 「功成事遂，百姓皆谓我自然」, whose
  百姓 cast members as the governed, which the owner did not want.
  成色 stands as a plain word (volume is what agents multiply), and guiding is carried by the opening scene. Drafts
  had used 《传习录》 on 精金 and 《学记》 「道而弗牵」, and the culture section 《论语·子路》 「其身正」; each restated what
  the prose already said. Not used: 57's 「法令滋彰」 (only the 王弼本 reads so); the 「九征」 of 《庄子·列御寇》, which
  tests people by traps. The rule is written once, in the prose rules' composition file.
- **Baselines as the guidelines hold them.** The four baselines are stated as the charter words them; the first and
  fourth together correspond to chapter 3's 「话只说到证据所能支持的程度」, the third to its 「结果有人负责」.
- **The book-wide pass in this Change.** The decision that the book does not treat people as objects covers every
  chapter, and the read of the others found thirteen places; they join this Change so that one review and one proof
  cover the whole decision.
- **接替 and 接手 for people, 更换 for agents.** A colleague takes over a colleague's work; an agent is swapped. Where
  both are meant, the doing side 并不固定 (`roles.typ`) and the parties 接替 each other. The rule is written once, in
  `human-voice.md` §1 and its table.
- **带宽 sentences.** 「这份有限的带宽用在哪里」 becomes the decision it stood for, which conclusions someone must
  understand and who; a person's positions are limited by what they can understand (以他弄得明白为限).
- **选人, support, rule first.** 选人 replaces 筛选, as the back matter names the point. Colleagues who need more
  support are owed the written standards and mentoring the chapter names; the difficulty of fairness stays in leniency
  itself, as 「标准不因人而降」 requires. The incentive rule opens its paragraph with 应当.
- **Qualities among the causes.** The owner set this on 2026-11-28: initiative and other good qualities are a cause
  and the work's results the effect; cultivation does not solve everything, and some qualities are reached by
  selection. The leading chapter places it in its argument: what a person achieves comes from ability, conditions and
  qualities; a period's assessment reads what was done, and the entry grade is set against the grade's written
  requirements, as the guidelines have it (interview evidence against the grade's description); the decision to hire
  also looks for qualities that cultivation can hardly supply, read from how a person has worked across several pieces
  of work before. Methods can be taught, the will to dig in hardly. So the book adds no second bar to the grade: the
  gate is the decision to hire, beside the entry grade. Whether the guidelines should name it is with the session
  aligning them (cross-project, O186), put to the owner as an option. Selection keeps the gate within hiring, and the
  paragraph on density ends with recruiting, cultivating and retaining together. §7 of the prose rules keeps its
  boundary for assessment, recognition, promotion and the entry grade, and lets qualities be named as causes where the
  leading chapter speaks of the decision to hire and of culture, never as labels that sort colleagues.
- **Culture.** The owner named team culture as important on the same day; the book had no word for it. It is derived
  here: what a team's people usually do where no rule reaches, above the floor the rules keep (how deep a check goes,
  whether one asks, whether one names a doubt in a colleague's result, whether a question is pursued to understanding),
  moments that multiply as agents return plausible results fast. It decides whether a person's will and abilities last
  after selection and cultivation, and it is shaped more by what the leader does than by what he says. It is its own section after the conditions a leader creates; its last sentence
  points to the fairness section for two of the leader's acts. A cold reading settled its examples so that none is a
  rule the book already states.
- **The review of the chapter's first draft** (`433661a`), commands and meaning, with a second reader of its findings, is
  answered in the chapter: the first baseline whole (不把不确定说成确定); who the decision owner is and that he carries the
  tier into the decisions it informs; the tier told in person at the period's end; what an improvement plan leaves unmet
  is a requirement it names; 专员's capability (给定方法中的步骤) beside his responsibility; one triad for 成色, the one
  chapter 31 points to, with 弄明白's third test (适用于什么范围); 认可 alone, as it includes rewards; S's next step;
  every task's agreed result (evolve: "every task still owes its agreed outcome"); the bands 法 and 道 as the guidelines
  name them; same grade and same records give the same tier, and no forced distribution; 不就……也不对外 for one 对 in
  two senses; the records that work done the book's way leaves; the pointers in `leading.typ` (晋升) and `improve.typ`
  (走到了哪里、下一步往哪里走) name what the chapter now holds; 成员 for an unanchored 他; and no colon inside the
  《道德经》 quotation that a colon introduces (GB/T 15834—2011 4.7.3.5); that quotation was later replaced. The chapter says the baselines correspond (对应) to chapter 3's two sayings, not that they are them.
- **The review of `23f87f3`** is answered: the period's goals leave what the tier is read against (`talent.typ:39`;
  goals stay where the period is planned and in pay, as the guidelines have them); the heading of `change.typ` no longer
  says the doer can be replaced (「动手的不再只是人」), which task 2.2's search had classed as said of agents; 选人 no
  longer stands as a bare coordinate beside 招聘 (see "Qualities among the causes" for its final form); the leader's three questions are named as his (`leading.typ:7`); and the band of 道, the two 也 and the
  improvement plan's condition are tightened (`talent.typ:18`, `:41`, `:47`). The colon of `leading.typ:45` stays: it
  states that the effort poured in is the leniency, as the chapter meant. `talent.typ:12` keeps 步骤 twice for the
  guidelines' two columns, and `:16` keeps the guidelines' words for the band.
- **The review of `8e1107b`** is answered: the decision to hire is told apart from the entry grade, which the
  guidelines set against the grade's description, and §7 says so; the fairness section's 回报和后果 correspond to
  (对应) two of the leader's acts, not equal them; 习惯 leaves the teachable side, since a quality is read from how a
  person consistently works; and wordings are tightened (培养出来, a full stop after 查得出来的部分, 不论深浅, 「因为有些品质……」, 「要看一个人以往……」).
- **The review of `ebe6b4b`** is answered: 成色 is no longer set against 分量, which elsewhere in the book means a
  person's worth; the editor chose 「而不只是多少和快慢」, since the sentence before says the three mean less and less,
  not nothing, and each piece of work still delivers its agreed result. Notes taken: the entry-grade clause gains its
  verb (也要对照), 「决定是否录用」, 「靠培养」, the quotation introduced as 「《道德经》说」 (a bare 三十三章 could read as a count, and 第三十三章 is the
  form the book keeps for its own chapters, derived by reference), and :60 names where the gap lies (那段说明是评审人补写的).

## Risks / Trade-offs

- A reader who needs a tier's conditions in detail must open the guidelines; the chapter says where.
- The one classical line is new to the book's register; it is glossed in plain Chinese.
