## Context

Chapter 29 followed the department's guidelines closely enough to be used as a procedure. The owner, on 2026-11-28, decided its purpose: helping members grow, with clear goals, not
measuring people; principles and spirit in the book, procedure in the guidelines; and, when a draft left the grades and
tiers out altogether, that they stay, explained as goals. A survey of Chinese classics on guiding, knowing and judging
people (Daoist, Confucian and Legalist texts, read from Wikisource with their commentaries) informed the positioning
and the narration.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** a chapter a leader and a member can both read for where the member stands and where to go next; the grades
and tiers stated as goals; the principles of assessment kept; no person treated as an object.

**Non-Goals:** changing any rule of the guidelines; 带宽 as a term, which names how much one person can understand in
thirty-one places and reads correctly there (a term changes everywhere or nowhere), so only the sentences that
allocated other people's understanding as a capacity are recast; the department's case facts in the leading chapter; a
release.

## Decisions

- **Purpose before mechanism.** The chapter opens on a period-end conversation and the four things a team does for a
  member's growth (state the goal, see what was done, recognise it, agree the next step); the grades and tiers follow as
  the goals that conversation refers to.
- **Grades in prose, by stage.** Each stage states its goal and what its two grades ask, in the guidelines' words
  shortened; a table would set the eight grades as gates to pass, while the stage's goal is what joins them.
- **Tiers read from B, each with its next step.** B is the grade's expected performance, C the pass, D below it; A and
  S presuppose B and look at the next grade. One sentence gives the next step each tier points to. The conditions in
  detail stay in the guidelines, so the book keeps one source of each.
- **成色 instead of the two sides.** The three things chapter 31 says stay with a person (弄明白、担得起、让别人也弄得明白)
  are what the chapter looks for, so the book names them once and both chapters agree.
- **Classics, three and exact.** 《传习录》 (成色, not 分两) because volume and speed are what an agent multiplies;
  《学记》 (道而弗牵) for guiding; 《道德经》 17 (王弼本) for the end. Not used: 57's 「法令滋彰」, which only the 王弼本
  reads so (the 河上公本, 郭店 and 帛书 have 法物); the 「九征」 of 《庄子·列御寇》, which tests people by traps; 「君子不器」,
  which would read as belittling the first stage.
- **Baselines as the guidelines hold them.** The four baselines are stated as the charter words them; the first and
  fourth together are chapter 3's 「话只说到证据所能支持的程度」, the third its 「结果有人负责」.
- **The book-wide pass in this Change.** The decision that the book does not treat people as objects covers every
  chapter, and the read of the others found thirteen places; they join this Change so that one review and one proof
  cover the whole decision.
- **接替 and 接手 for people, 更换 for agents.** A colleague takes over a colleague's work; an agent is swapped. Where
  both are meant, the doing side 并不固定 (`roles.typ`) and the parties 接替 each other. The rule is written once, in
  `human-voice.md` §1 and its table.
- **带宽 sentences.** 「这份有限的带宽用在哪里」 becomes the decision it stood for, which conclusions someone must
  understand and who; a person's positions are limited by what they can understand (以他弄得明白为限).
- **选人, support, rule first.** 选人 replaces 筛选, as the back matter names the point; 主动钻研的意愿 stays as the
  experience it rests on (the prose rules' boundary that judging rests on work done governs a member's assessment).
  Colleagues who need more support are owed the written standards and mentoring the chapter names; the difficulty of
  fairness stays in leniency itself, as 「标准不因人而降」 requires. The incentive rule opens its paragraph with 应当.
- **The review of the chapter's first draft** (`433661a`), commands and meaning, with a second reader of its findings, is
  answered in the chapter: the first baseline whole (不把不确定说成确定); who the decision owner is and that he carries the
  tier into the decisions it informs; the tier told in person at the period's end; what an improvement plan leaves unmet
  is a requirement it names; 专员's capability (给定方法中的步骤) beside his responsibility; one triad for 成色, the one
  chapter 31 points to, with 弄明白's third test (适用于什么范围); 认可 alone, as it includes rewards; S's next step;
  every task's agreed result (evolve: "every task still owes its agreed outcome"); the bands 法 and 道 as the guidelines
  name them; same grade and same records give the same tier, and no forced distribution; 不就……也不对外 for one 对 in
  two senses; the records that work done the book's way leaves; the pointers in `leading.typ` (晋升) and `improve.typ`
  (走到了哪里、下一步往哪里走) name what the chapter now holds; 成员 for an unanchored 他; and no colon inside the
  《道德经》 quotation that a colon introduces (GB/T 15834—2011 4.7.3.5), the inner colon being the edition's
  punctuation, not the text's. The two baselines that chapter 3 states are said to correspond to it (对应), not to be it.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- A reader who needs a tier's conditions in detail must open the guidelines; the chapter says where.
- The classics are new to the book's register; each is glossed in plain Chinese and used once.
