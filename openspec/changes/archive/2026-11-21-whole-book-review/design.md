## Context

See [proposal.md](proposal.md). The standard every reviewer applied is `build/evidence/whole-book-review/standard.md`.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Each claim follows from stated premises and is no stronger than its support; each term keeps one meaning; each
  section's order serves its argument; each sentence is standard written Chinese, clear on first reading.

**Non-Goals:**

- New facts, new cases or new exercises; changing printed commands; renumbering chapters (the part-three order stays,
  with its reason stated where the reader meets it).

## Decisions

- One editor per part applies both lenses' findings to its own files, so no two editors write one file; repairs that
  cross parts are assigned to one editor with the exact text to receive or remove.
- A finding's proposed sentence is a lead: a correction that needs a fact is written from the record it rests on, or
  left conservative and listed as unresolved.
- Where reviewers disagree, the standard decides, and the decision is recorded beside the finding.
- After the repairs, readers who have not seen the edits read the changed passages against the standard.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Wide edits drift from the records] → No new facts; each editor lists every finding with its disposition; fresh
  readers check the changed passages.
- [Repairs pull the text toward the reviewers' register] → The owner's own judgements on earlier sentences are the
  calibration, not the reviewers' proposals.
