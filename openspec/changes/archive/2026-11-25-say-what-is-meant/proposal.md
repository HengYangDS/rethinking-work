## Why

Chapter 29 listed traits that a tier does not judge, after the guidelines did; the owner said on 2026-11-25 that
judging rests on work performance and that naming anything else, even as an exclusion, plants a meaning nobody gave
(19:39–19:52, relayed by the guidelines' session: 「本来不提就好了，讲基于工作表现评价就好」「全局性地，请避免：1. 狗尾续貂
2. 越描越黑 3. 脱裤子放屁」). The guidelines' fix deletes the sentence and adds nothing (DDWG 10.1.2). A whole-book read
found the same habit elsewhere: denials of readings the text never suggested, closing restatements and explanations of
what is already plain.

## What Changes

- Chapter 29 and chapter 28 stop naming what a judgement is not: the trait list, 「不是整个人」, 「产出代表不了一个人的
  价值」, 「不计产出」 and the remark on faking go; the chapters say what is judged.
- Across the book, 37 places are cut: a denial of a meaning nobody gave, a sentence that repeats the point just made, or
  an explanation of the obvious. Where deletion alone leaves a sentence broken, the least wording that joins it is used.
- Places that state the guidelines' own rules, correct a misreading the text itself sets up, or carry a chapter's point
  stay.
