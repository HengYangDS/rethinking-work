## Context

See [proposal.md](proposal.md). A reader that had not written the text read all 38 book files for the three faults and
listed 47 candidates with its confidence (2026-11-25 20:1x). The editor read each in its paragraph.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Each passage says what it means once and stops.

**Non-Goals:**

- Rewording beyond the cut; the passages are read again in 0.2.7's whole-book reading.

## Decisions

- Cut by deletion. Two places needed joining words: 「把同事的话也算作线索，是因为问题出在转述上」 and 「评定等级记的是一段时间里
  的表现」; 「局部指标也代表不了」 loses 「也」 because the sentence it echoed is gone.
- Kept, with the reason: chapter 29's 「评定等级不改动一件工作的分」, 「不指速度，也不指工具用得熟」 and 「在岗的年限……替代不
  了」 are the guidelines' own rules (DDWG keeps them); 「新颖、完整、别人都在用」 answers reasons readers do give; the
  opening scene of the context chapter sets up 「是否敷衍」; the diagnosis chapter's 「没有给出重装……的理由」 says what not
  to do yet; two practice answers correct the conclusion their own result invites; 「对事不对人」 is a review rule; the
  change chapter's one-line paragraph and the standing-rules chapter's last sentence carry their point; the team
  chapter's 「劳动可以少分担，弄明白却仍然离不开别人」 is what the next paragraph takes up; the tools chapter's opening
  says what a model without tools cannot reach.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- A cut may remove a qualification a reader needed. The 0.2.7 whole-book reading reads every changed passage, and a
  cut it finds wanting is restored there.
