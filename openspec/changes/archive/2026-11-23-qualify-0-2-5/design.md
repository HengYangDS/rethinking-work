## Context

See [proposal.md](proposal.md). The procedure is the one `docs/publication-acceptance.md` names under "Check complete
meaning": parts of about thirty pages, a reader each who did not write it, then repairs, then a different reader of the
changed passages and the commands review.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Every A and every verifiable 信 finding repaired; B and 术语 repaired unless the change would make the sentence worse.
- No repair adds a fact that the book, the exercise pack or the run records do not hold.

**Non-Goals:**

- Re-deciding passages the owner settled, or the release itself.

## Decisions

- Each editor answered every finding 改, 不改 (with a reason) or 待核 (outside knowledge not in the records); I reviewed
  each proposal and recorded every skipped edit with its reason (`build/evidence/qualify-0.2.5/edits/decisions.md`).
- A finding whose repair needs a fact not on record stays as it is and is listed, rather than filled by inference.
- Page room is made by the reviewed reserve and by shortening the repairs' own added words, not by layout changes.

- [A repair changes meaning] → A reader who did not write the repairs reads every changed sentence against its source,
  and the commands reviewer reads the prose beside printed commands against the run records.
