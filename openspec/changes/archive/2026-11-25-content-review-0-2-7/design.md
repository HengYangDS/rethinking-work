## Context

See [proposal.md](proposal.md). Two editors who had not written the passages applied the reviews' findings to the two
halves of the book under one set of rules (`build/evidence/release-0-2-7/content/RULES.md`); the coordinating editor
read their reports and the diff, and refitted the pages.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Each fact told once, in the place that owns it; the practice pack with one reader index.

**Non-Goals:**

- Appendix D's counts and claims: the commands reviewer checks every printed command against run records for this
  edition. Chapter 29's rules: they follow DDWG 10.1.2 and are only compressed, not reworded. Naming chapter 3's two
  unnamed conditions. Removing figures 2 and 6, which the reviews proposed: the owner reviewed the figures' look the
  same day, so they are corrected instead.

## Decisions

- Kept against a proposal, where a fact would otherwise be lost: chapter 17's two rules for the runs, chapter 20's
  「由主会话代为派出的也算他派出」, chapter 18's cause of the night-time failure (chapter 7 points to it), appendix D's
  revision sentence (it holds how two corrected claims were re-run) and its language-server sentence (chapter 10 rests
  on it).
- Moved to appendix D: the condition that the sub-agent was not told the marker word (chapter 20's experiment).
- Restored after review: 「并非每项工作都要做得突出，但」 with the four marks, because it answers the misreading the
  marks invite; chapter 19 keeps 「没有理由重装客户端」 rather than an instruction not to.
- Refits by wording, not by spacing: a split lead-in in chapter 11, a single-line figure box in practice 1, shorter
  sentences in chapters 12, 18, 19 and 26.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- A cut may remove a qualification a reader needed. 0.2.7's whole-book reading by both families reads every page, and
  the commands reviewer checks the printed commands; a loss they find is restored there.
