## Why

The owner found the book silent on how talent is judged (「我觉得全书，对于人才的判断和评价标准，是缺失的」, 2026-11-22
14:30) and asked for a standard of tiers S to E for the agentic era, joined to the department's professional sequence
and serving long-term direction, fair assessment, development and selection (14:32–14:36); he allowed an expert
committee. Its two rounds and his rulings (14:52–16:24) fixed the standard: two sides, work and talent; a tier is a
period's performance at the member's own grade; eight grades in four bands; one source in the department's guidelines,
which the book states in its own words. He also locked the whole book at 200 pages (14:52), so the new chapter's room
comes from removing redundancy.

## What Changes

- A chapter after chapter 28 states the standard: the work score, the tier and the grade and their one-way chain; what
  the tier judges and what it does not; the sequence in four bands and eight grades; the six tiers at one's own grade;
  where evidence comes from; the five steps of assessment; a worked judgement; promotion, the two crossings and hiring.
  Chapters 29 and 30 become 30 and 31; the numbers printed in other chapters follow.
- Passages that say again what another place says better are cut or reduced to a pointer, by the readings saved in
  `build/evidence/page-budget/redundancy/`; no printed command, case date, qualification or exercise is cut.
- The layout declares the edition's page ceiling, and a test fails when the PDF exceeds it.
