## Why

The owner asked that the whole book attend to philosophical depth, care for people, technical thinking, higher-level
thinking about the change of the era and judgement of the coming paradigm, understood from their essence, and that it
hold the invariants amid change and respond actively (2026-11-28). Five readers, one lens each, and the editor's own
reading found the same gap: the book derives what does not change to the ground but leaves what may change as one
sentence, so the change reads as loss and risk, the forward view is withheld, people appear mostly as functions, and
the ending turns inward instead of rising.

## What Changes

- The root chapter `什么没变` says what understanding is (a model held for a use, checked against the record, aware of
  its bounds, which the five questions of the 一个人 part ask), and why it cannot be handed over, between people too.
- It replaces 「容易走偏的三处」 with the active half: many practices were never conditions but vouched for one while
  making was dear; cheap making cuts them loose; the response is to hold the condition and replace what vouched for it.
  The later parts' reforms are named as instances, and the reader is given the method for changes the book does not
  name.
- It says what cheap making should buy first (the bought part of understanding, what could not be afforded before),
  adds a section on where people's work is heading, derived from the two steps that cannot be bought, with its
  overturning conditions, and reads the 可逆 criterion in both directions: strict for what cannot be undone, fast and parallel for what can.
- The agent chapter adds that models are also trained toward what was rewarded (people's preference, passing checks)
  and what follows: a model may satisfy a test rather than the intent. Its mitigations are stated as one principle:
  what must hold moves to the side that executes deterministically. Its 哪些会变 section joins the 六个性质 and
  layer sections, since where people's work is heading is now derived in the root.
- Small mechanisms are added where a decision turns on them: longer context is used less well (context), errors
  compound over steps (run), a twenty-item evaluation resolves only large differences and unattended runs need right k
  times in a row (evaluate), several agents multiply tokens (orchestrate).
- Two passages that rested on what agents fail to do rest on structure instead (change, roles).
- People: the growth chapter names what agents and records can and cannot take, says a veteran's skill is not voided,
  trains judgement from the first year, and cites the 1983 concern that skills decay under automation and that the
  most successful automated systems may most need investment in people; the leading chapter names protecting members' bandwidth as the leader's most concrete
  care and restates fairness and reward against the stated standard rather than between people; the review chapter
  protects whoever stops when unsure; the improve chapter's close states the rhythm that follows from 可逆.
- The 序 states both halves of the stance; the 结语 draws the reshaped work and ends on what a person who chooses to
  understand leaves.
- The appendix's basis table gains the forward judgement and the training fact; a short list names the public sources
  the text cites.
