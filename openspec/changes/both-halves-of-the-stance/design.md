## Context

On 2026-11-28 the owner asked that the whole book attend to philosophical depth, care for people, technical thinking,
higher-level thinking about the change of the era and judgement of the coming paradigm, understood from their essence
rather than literally, and that it hold the invariants amid change and respond actively. The owner confirmed the
editor's reading the same day: the first request is a demand on the depth of the argument, not a list of topics, so
what it asks is derived from the book's premises and placed where the argument turns, with no new chapters and no
added words for their own sake; the second is the book's stance toward the change, whose missing half is actively
redesigning work and team at today's cost, derived from the same invariants.

The editor derived the gap from the manuscript first; five readers, each given one lens (philosophy, care for people,
technical thinking, the era, the stance), the manuscript and the owner's words but not the editor's reading, found the
same gap: the root derives what does not change to the ground but leaves what may change as one sentence, so change
reads as loss and risk, the forward view is withheld, people appear mostly as functions, and the closing turns inward.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** the root derives both halves of the stance; a reader can apply its method to changes the book does not
name; a forward judgement states its bases and what would overturn it; the mechanisms a decision turns on are given
with their calculation and assumptions; people are treated as people where the text discusses them; the edition stays
within 200 pages and its layout converges.

**Non-Goals:** new chapters or parts; topics added for their own sake; predictions resting on what agents cannot yet
do; a release.

## Decisions

- **Conditions and what vouched for them.** The four conditions say what a result must achieve, not how. Many
  practices (a thorough report, making the thing oneself, a colleague one can ask, hiring by skill, scheduling by
  effort, weekly reports of work done) vouched for a condition while making was dear. Cheap making cuts the link, so
  the response is to hold the condition and replace what vouched for it. The later parts' reforms (team by problem
  domain, run, talent, growth) are named as instances, and three questions let a reader treat a change the book does
  not name. This replaces the section on three ways to go wrong; its first point becomes the method, its second is
  covered by the bounds chapter, its third (the cost of writing things down) is kept.
- **Understanding as a model held for a use.** Understanding is defined once, from the model chapter: a model built for
  a use, checked against the original record, aware of where it may fail; the five questions of the 一个人 part ask
  exactly these. Making once built it as a by-product; output only has to meet a form, so making no longer forces
  understanding, and no one can understand for another, between people as with agents.
- **What cheap making buys first.** The bought part of understanding (listing causes, checking every result,
  independent agents working from the original) and what could not be afforded before (frequent full comparisons,
  parallel prototypes, a time-boxed exploration). Spending the saving only on more output makes the review bottleneck
  worse.
- **Where people's work gathers.** If models keep improving, the bought part grows; the two steps that cannot be bought
  (first declaring a kind of case an error, reading a result and answering for it) do not shrink. So people's work
  gathers at four places: deciding what counts as right and naming the first error; writing understanding into reasons
  and checks; reading results and answering for them, with what cannot be undone decided before acting; training
  people who can do these. Each item comes from the two steps or from the two classes checks cannot cover, and the
  closing names the same four. How much can be handed over without watching each result depends on the understanding
  made into checks, as the verify chapter credits checks, not reasons, with that. The judgement
  rests on the chapter's four facts and the two grounds of 责任交不出去, not on present limits of agents, and is to be revisited when
  one of them changes; the appendix's basis table carries it. The verb is 定为 (a decision), not 认出 (a capability).
- **可逆 in both directions.** Irreversible steps keep strict procedure; steps that can be undone and touch little may go
  fast and in parallel, since the criterion weighs both points (the boundaries chapter asks first before reversible
  writes to shared places). The improve chapter's close states the rhythm that follows: trials fast, switching slow.
- **Training toward reward.** The agent chapter says what a model is trained toward (what reads as plausible, what
  people prefer, what passes checks) and that these mostly coincide with being right; a vendor's system card reports
  special-casing or editing tests as reward hacking from reinforcement learning, mostly after several failed attempts,
  with that condition kept. The mitigations become one principle: what must hold moves to the side the host executes
  without the model's judgement. Its 哪些会变 section joins the 六个性质 paragraph (the sixth property is the one most
  likely to change) and the layer section (the host takes over work), since the forward view now lives in the root.
- **Mechanisms where a decision turns.** Longer context is used less well (two public studies); errors compound over
  steps (0.95 to the twentieth, about 0.36, assuming independent errors and no correction); a twenty-item evaluation
  has a standard error of about sixteen points for a difference near one half, so smaller differences need more items
  or item-by-item comparison, and an unattended schedule needs right k times in a row (0.9 to the fifth, about 0.59,
  assuming independent runs); one agent uses about four times the tokens of a chat and the vendor's multi-agent system
  about fifteen. Each source was read at its origin.
- **People.** The growth chapter names what agents and records can take and what they cannot, says a veteran's skill
  is not voided, trains judgement from the first year, and cites Bainbridge (1983) with its own hedges (a concern;
  perhaps). The leading chapter names protecting members' bandwidth as the leader's most concrete care, and states
  fairness and reward against the stated standard, with a time-bound plan for whoever needs support. The review
  chapter protects whoever stops when unsure. The change and roles chapters rest on structure, not on what agents fail
  to do.
- **Opening and closing.** The 序 states both halves: cheap making lets practices set for dear making be rearranged;
  the four conditions do not change. The 结语 draws the direction of work while the root's bases hold and ends on what
  a person who chooses to understand leaves to the next.
- **Sources.** A short list in the back matter names each public source the text cites, with the chapter that cites
  it. The Epoch AI price series was not read and is not used.
- **Dropped.** A sentence in the shared chapter on not assessing people by intervention counts: the improve chapter
  already says a metric does not replace the work done, and the sentence also set off the layout oscillation below.
- **Reviews before the lane.** A reader who had not seen the work read draft 1 (nineteen points) and another read
  draft 3; both found the argument derived from the root. The second reader's points were taken, among them: the
  forward judgement names both grounds of 责任交不出去 among its bases; a sentence saying other teams can buy the same
  capability was removed, as nothing in the chapter derives it; the closing keeps the root's premise and drops a
  prediction of how much work agents will do; the practice of asking a colleague is cut loose by agents that cannot
  be asked, not by cheap making, and the framework says so; reward for passing checks cites DeepSeek-R1 (§2.2.2),
  and test special-casing is attributed to one model's system card, with METR's observation on other models beside
  it. One point was declined: 作保 stays rather than 凭据, because the practices did more than serve as evidence (a
  colleague one could ask stood in for writing things down), while 凭据 names evidence only.
- **A sentence-level reading.** A third reader read every changed sentence of draft 4 for clarity; its points were
  taken, chiefly: one wording for the first error throughout (判定第一次遇到的情形算不算错), 理解 written into 理由和检查
  instead of 弄明白的结果, which collided with the book's word for output, and a preface whose one subject is plain.
- **The review of the committed lane.** The commands reviewer and two further readers read the first commit and the
  layout fix: no finding on the commands; three findings, all settled: the evaluation chapter's standard deviation
  states its independence assumption, the list of where people's work gathers keeps both unbuyable steps (and the
  closing names the same items), and 可逆 lets only what can be undone and touches little go fast. Of sixteen notes,
  fourteen were taken: Arabic 百分点 and one unit in the evaluation numbers, the preface's summary of chapters 20 and 21
  restored, 依据 rather than 前提 in the closing, the appendix lead naming its sources, Western quotation marks for
  English titles, and formulas kept on one line, among others. Two were left, with a reason: 作保 for 显性 (asking
  stood in for writing down, as above) and the two senses of 模型 in the root chapter, which the context separates.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **Layout.** Draft 2 built to 202 pages and did not converge: with the three agent-chapter edits together, the
  chapter's closing box fell alone onto a new page and the layout oscillated (page counter, code-block geometry,
  heading positions); each edit alone converged. Concise rewriting in the agent, evaluate and orchestrate chapters
  brought the edition to 198 pages with no warning (the orchestrate overflow came from the 编排 heading and its
  unbreakable table moving a page). The reserve is two pages. `tests/test_layout.py` on the first commit found three
  layout faults: a table cell ending with one character alone, a justified line in the sources list opened past the
  bound, and the evaluation config block split with two lines on its second page. Three wording changes fixed them;
  one restores the base's wording of the Phoenix sentence.
- **Ageing sources.** The cited studies and system card date from 2023 to 2025; the text uses them as mechanisms with
  their conditions, and the forward judgement does not rest on them.
- **Numbers.** Each added number shows its calculation and its assumptions, so a reader can tell when it does not
  apply.
