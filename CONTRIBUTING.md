# Contributing

This repository maintains one Chinese publication. A contribution improves its
argument, teaching cases, diagrams, exercises, presentation or verification.
It does not configure a reader's workstation or grant operational authority.

Follow the [editing and build journey](docs/contributing.md). The
[publication acceptance guide](docs/publication-acceptance.md) explains what must
be checked before a candidate becomes a delivered edition.

Use the installed ETHOS `status` result and its version-matched guidance to select
an owned Work Lane, exact prewrite admission and the required proof. Official
OpenSpec owns the selected Change's intent and tasks. Do not bypass hooks, infer
landing permission from a passing test or publish remotely without authorization.

Keep the manuscript Chinese and new contributor guidance English. Public examples
must remain synthetic or explicitly licensed. Preserve facts, source roles,
qualifications and old editions; a smoother sentence cannot erase an unknown.
An author's name in a PDF is attribution, not a certificate-based signature.
